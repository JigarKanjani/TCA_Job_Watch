# Signal Field — the hero's generative philosophy

**Movement: Signal Field.** A job search is a field of noise with rare signal in it.
Hundreds of postings drift through the market every hour; almost none are yours.
The hero renders that truth as a living system: a slow sweep crossing a field of
drifting points, igniting only the few that match.

**The field.** Points are postings. Each is born at a seeded position, fades in,
drifts along a smooth value-noise flow (never straight lines — markets meander),
and quietly expires, to be replaced elsewhere. Density scales with the canvas area
so the field reads the same on a phone and a monitor. Nothing is random at run
time: one seed fully determines the composition, so every visit opens on the same
calm, considered frame.

**The sweep.** A single arm rotates on a fixed period, trailing a conic wash of the
accent hue. The sweep is the algorithm's only clock. When it crosses a point the
point answers: ordinary postings brighten faintly and settle back; matches ignite
amber, throw a ripple ring, and hold an afterglow whose half-life is tuned so a
match is still warm when the arm comes round again. Being first is the product,
so the glow is brightest in the moment of discovery and decays from there.

**Restraint.** Matches are kept rare (a few percent) so each ignition means
something. Range rings are hairlines. Two colors only — the teal of the system,
the amber of a find — read from the page's own theme tokens. Motion is slow
enough to sit behind reading. The craft is in the tuning: drift speed, sweep
period, glow half-life and match rarity were balanced so the field feels alive
without ever asking for attention.

**Conceptual seed.** The sweep period compresses a day into seconds and the five
range rings are the five workdays; a posting found in its first hour is the
brightest thing on screen. Those who've lived the job hunt will feel it; everyone
else just sees a quiet radar finding signal in noise.

**Accessibility.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the field renders one still frame
with a few lit matches and never animates. The loop pauses when the hero is
offscreen or the tab is hidden.
